## 2024-05-27 - Hoisting collection literals to constants
**Learning:** Hoisting collection literals (sets, lists, dicts) used in membership checks or mappings to module-level or class-level constants avoids the overhead of rebuilding the collection on every function call. In Python 3.12+, inline list literals in membership checks are optimized to constant tuples, making them faster than inline set literals for small collections due to set construction cost. In `CompositeFSM`, this reduces the execution time of `get_execution_outcome` and `can_transition` by roughly a factor of 10x and 2x respectively.
**Action:** When performing membership checks or dictionary lookups inside frequently called methods, define the collection as a module-level or class-level constant tuple or dictionary.
