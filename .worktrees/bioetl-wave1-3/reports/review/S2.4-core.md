### AP-001/DI-001: Hard-coded Constructor Dependencies -- ANALYSIS

**`BasePipeline.__init__` (base.py:103):**
- `self._shutdown_signal = ShutdownSignal()` -- Creates a `ShutdownSignal` dataclass (defined in `application/core/shutdown.py`). This is a lightweight coordination primitive (asyncio.Event wrapper), not an external service. **EXC-015 (Config/VO defaults) -- NOT a violation.**

**`BatchExecutor.__init__` (batch_executor.py:129-195):**
- Creates `BatchMemoryManager`, `BatchMetricsRecorder`, `BatchTransformer`, `BatchWriter`, `BatchTracingManager`, and `QuarantineManager` internally.
- **Analysis:** All created objects are application-layer internal components that:
  1. Take their dependencies from the constructor-injected `services`, `config`, `context`, and `tracer` parameters
  2. Are purely internal decomposition of BatchExecutor's responsibilities
  3. Have no external I/O themselves -- they delegate to injected ports
  4. Are not independently testable units that need mocking at boundaries
- **Verdict: BORDERLINE but ACCEPTABLE.** This is internal composition (delegation pattern), not hard-coded external dependencies. The real dependencies (StoragePort, MetricsPort, etc.) are properly injected via `PipelineServices`. The internal components are implementation details. Per EXC-005, files with delegation calls are not god objects.

**`RecordProcessor.__init__` (record_processor.py:74-93):**
- Same pattern as BatchExecutor -- creates `BatchMetricsRecorder`, `BatchTransformer`, `BatchWriter` internally from injected dependencies.
- **Same verdict as BatchExecutor: ACCEPTABLE.**

**`LockManager.__init__` (lock_manager.py:220):**
- `self._heartbeat = HeartbeatTask(...)` -- Creates a heartbeat coroutine wrapper from injected dependencies.
- **ACCEPTABLE** -- internal coordination component.
