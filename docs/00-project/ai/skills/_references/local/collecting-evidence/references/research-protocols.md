# Research Protocols

## Goal
Collect evidence that is reproducible, source-traceable, and decision-ready.

## Minimum Protocol
1. Define the question in one sentence.
2. Collect at least two independent sources.
3. Capture exact claim, context, and retrieval date.
4. Record uncertainty and assumptions.
5. Link evidence to a pillar and decision candidate.

## Source Quality Heuristic
- Official docs / primary data: high confidence.
- Peer-reviewed / technical reports: medium-high confidence.
- Blog posts / social content: medium-low confidence unless corroborated.
