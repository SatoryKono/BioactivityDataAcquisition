# Analyst Guidance

## Core Rules

1. Evaluate patterns in the text before researching factual accuracy.
1. Quote the evidence that justifies each elevated score.
1. Always generate both manipulative and legitimate interpretations.
1. Escalate to deep research when the trigger thresholds are met.

## Guidance By Composite Factor

### Emotional Manipulation

- Check whether emotional load replaces concrete reasoning.
- Separate concern-driven language from deliberate escalation.

### Suspicious Timing

- Look for narratives that gain force from event timing rather than evidence quality.
- Check if omitted timeline details would materially change interpretation.

### Uniform Messaging

- Notice repeated slogans, phrasing, or synchronized framing.
- Compare attribution verbs and whether they differ by favored side.

### Tribal Division

- Watch for identity pressure, loyalty tests, and dehumanizing shorthand.
- Distinguish ordinary disagreement from explicit out-group construction.

### Missing Information

- Ask what a reasonable reader would need to know to judge fairly.
- Trigger verification when authority, sourcing, or context is weak.
